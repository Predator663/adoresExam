How it behaves: she starts at medium. Each right answer to a hard question lifts her level a lot, each miss on an easy one lowers it a lot.
The next question is one just above her level, from her weakest topic (from her published exam results plus how she is doing in this quiz).
Never repeats a question, never asks one topic 3 times in a row, never changes questions she already answered, never sends answers to the phone.
Normal quizzes, the daily challenge and offline packs are unchanged.
