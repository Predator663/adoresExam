How it works
- A student picks a classmate (same class, not hidden from the leaderboard), a subject, 5/10/15 questions, 15-90 seconds per question and
  6/24/48/72 hours for it to be finished. The classmate gets a notification. At most 3 waiting at once, one open tournament per pair.
- Both get the SAME questions, each in her own order with her own option order. The challenger can play now; the classmate any time
  before the deadline.
- Timer: kept by the server. The clock starts when a question is first shown; closing the app does not stop it; a late answer (limit + 2.5 s for
  slow networks) scores 0. Correct answers and the other player's score are hidden until it is settled.
- Settled once, when both finished or at the deadline. Higher score wins; equal score -> faster total time; times within 1 s -> draw.
  Only one finished before the deadline -> "no contest": nobody wins, the finisher gets the 15 XP play reward only. Declined/cancelled: no XP.
- XP: 15 for finishing + 35 for a win (+15 for a draw). At most 5 tournaments a day pay XP, so it cannot be farmed. Level, streak day and badges
  use the normal engines. Result screen: winner, scores, XP, new badges, question by question.
- Never touches exam marks, results or teachers' settings.

---- Arena redesign + any-class challenges (added) ----
- No more list of students. The challenger SEARCHES (2+ letters, max 8 results, optional class filter chips) by the name
  classmates see (chosen name, else "Amina K."). Hidden students are not found. Recent opponents show for one-tap rematches.
  GET /api/tournaments/search/?q=ne&form=3 . /options/ no longer returns "classmates".
- Any class can be challenged. CLASS STAKES (frozen when declared, shown before sending and in the invite pop-up):
    each class apart = 1 step, max 3.  Beat a HIGHER class: win XP +50% per step.  Beat a LOWER class: win XP -25% per step (min 30%).
    LOSE to a LOWER class: -10 XP per step (never below 0 total XP, never capped by the daily limit). Lose to a higher class: nothing.
  Questions come from the LOWER of the two classes. Tune with constants in gamification/tournaments.py (WIN_UP, WIN_DOWN, WIN_FLOOR, LOSE_COST).
